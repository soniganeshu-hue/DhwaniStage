Rental Terms, Equipment Guidelines & Viva Reference

How is the Refundable Security Deposit calculated and refunded?

Every equipment item has a predetermined refundable security deposit (e.g., ₹4,000 for a 1200W Dual Top PA speaker, ₹1,500 for a dual wireless mic set). The total deposit is collected at equipment pickup. Upon return, the sound technician verifies the gear condition on the Return Checklist. If all items are intact, 100% of the deposit is refunded on the spot. For minor wear, 20% is deducted; for damaged or missing gear, 100% of the allocated item deposit is forfeited.

What multi-day discounts are applied automatically?

1-Day events are billed at standard daily rates. 3-Day Weekend Festivals receive an automatic 15% discount on the base rental. Custom duration events of 3 days or longer receive an automatic 10% discount on base equipment rental.

What electrical precautions are required for high-wattage PA speakers?

High-wattage PA amplifiers require a dedicated 15-Ampere single-phase power socket with clean earthing (grounding) or a stabilized diesel generator (DG) set. Never plug heavy subwoofers into daisy-chained domestic 5A multi-plugs.

First-Semester Viva Quick Explain: How is data persisted?

All catalog stock, selected rental rig items, and confirmed booking records are stored in the browser's localStorage API using JSON.stringify() and retrieved using JSON.parse(). When you refresh the page or close the browser, your bookings and custom rigs remain intact with zero backend server dependencies!
